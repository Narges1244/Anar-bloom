# Anar Bloom Pricing

Price calculator and invoice maker for Anar Bloom: cakes, desserts, balloon and party decoration, custom orders, and branded PDF invoices.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages (free)

1. Sign in at https://github.com (create a free account if you don't have one).
2. Click **+** (top right) → **New repository**.
   - Repository name: `anar-bloom`
   - Choose **Public** (free GitHub Pages needs a public repository; your prices and invoices are NOT stored in it, see below).
   - Click **Create repository**.
3. On the new repository page, click **uploading an existing file**.
4. Drag in ALL the files from this folder (`index.html`, `logo.jpg`, `favicon.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.jpg`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `README.md`) and click **Commit changes**.
5. Go to **Settings** → **Pages** (left menu).
   - Source: **Deploy from a branch**
   - Branch: **main**, folder **/ (root)** → **Save**.
6. Wait 1–2 minutes and refresh. Your site will be at:
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/anar-bloom/`

## Use it like an app on your phone

- iPhone (Safari): open the site → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
- Android (Chrome): open the site → ⋮ menu → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.

## Where your data is saved

Your prices, orders and invoices are saved in the browser on each device, not on GitHub.
To move them between your phone and computer, open **Price list → Backup and move your data**:
**Download backup file** (or **Copy backup text**) on one device, then **Restore from backup** on the other.
Make a backup now and then so nothing is lost if the browser data is cleared.

## Updating the site later

Upload a new `index.html` to the repository (Add file → Upload files → Commit). The site updates in a minute or two. Your saved data stays in your browser.
